# Qodo brand assets

The Qodo mark used in the contribution diagrams comes from the [official media downloads page](https://www.qodo.ai/media-downloads/), using the logo displayed on that page. The mark identifies proposed integration roles; it does not imply that every marked capability is available today or that Qodo owns the organization's adoption and release decisions.

The original mark is preserved in `assets/qodo-logo.svg`. A / T / I labels are diagram annotations for agent, tool, and infrastructure contributions. These are not official product or emoji names. Qodo's name and mark remain its brand assets; the repository's CC0 license does not grant trademark rights.
